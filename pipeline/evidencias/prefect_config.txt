4. SCHEDULE CONFIGURADO
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Modo de registro:
    python orchestration/flows.py --serve

Chamada de serve() em flows.py:
    pipeline_licitei.serve(
        name="pipeline-licitei-diario",
        cron="0 6 * * *",
        tags=["pipeline", "pncp", "licitei", "medallion"],
    )

Interpretacao do cron "0 6 * * *":
    Execucao diaria as 06:00 UTC
    (equivale a 03:00 BRT / 04:00 BRST)
